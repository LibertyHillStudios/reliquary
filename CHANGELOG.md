# Reliquary

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-12)

- **Guild and friends see a new character from the start.** A character you log in with for the first time shares everything with your guild and your friends, and nothing with anyone else; a line in chat says so, and `/rq share` or the Sharing tab of `/rq` changes it. Share with anyone and they see its gear, talents, professions and character sheet until you choose more. A new install shares its prices and sources with the guild too. Characters you already have keep exactly what they shared.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-11)

- **A window of its own.** `/rq` is drawn in Reliquary's own look: dark ink, thin gold lines, its tabs as words along the top with a gold marker that slides to the one you choose, and each page laid out on cards. It moves a little as you use it: it opens and closes with a short fade, a page slides in when you change tab, the gold bar glides to the row you pick, bars fill and numbers count up, and a timeline stop eases open. Nothing makes you wait, and "Animations" on the Sharing tab turns the motion off. Drag the corner at the bottom right to make the window larger or smaller (double-click it for its own size); it keeps its size and where you put it.
- **Everything in the game window.** `/rq` holds all that Reliquary records, so nothing needs a web page:
  - **Characters**, the first tab: every character on your account, the one you're playing first, each with every part of it whatever it shares. Its page follows the game as you play: loot, gold, a new zone.
  - **Talents**: the three trees side by side, each on its own painting with its name and points over it: a learned talent in colour with its rank on a small plate (gold when learned in full, green in part), one not learned grey, the arrows between them, and its spell and rank on hover; your Legacy below, with what it has spent and has to spend. A guildmate's or friend's page shows the talents they learned, tree by tree.
  - **Gear read at a glance**: every slot with its item's name in its quality's colour beside it and its item level on its corner. Between them, under an arch, stands the game's own model of the character you're playing (drag to turn it), or another character's class crest, over their item level. **The character sheet** is one card at the right: professions on their bars, then General (health, mana, experience, rested, time played, this level, hearthstone), Attributes (with their base on hover), Weapons, Modifiers, Defense and Resistance.
  - **Timeline**: each stop a card on a line down the page, with its zone, its day and hours and what happened there; it opens to the quests handed in there, the Legacy challenges and the zones passed through, and the newest open themselves while the page has room. Before Reliquary's groups fold the same way, each saying how much it holds.
  - **Reputation and quests**: a card for each of the game's reputation groups, every faction on a bar in its standing's colour, and a card for each zone's quests, each with its level, its objectives and how far along they are.
  - **Bags and bank**: each bag a card under its name and icon with how full it is, every slot drawn, the empty ones too, and the totals at the foot. The keyring shows the rows with keys, and keys don't count as bag space.
  - **Recipes**: each profession a card with its skill on a bar and its recipes under it (click its name to fold it away), and beside them the recipe chosen: what it makes and each reagent with how many you have of how many it takes, every one a click from its page.
- **A button on the minimap**, among your other addons' buttons and drawn the same way: click it to open the window, right-click for Sharing, drag it around the edge. Reliquary is in the minimap's Addons menu too. "Show the minimap button" on the Sharing tab (and in Options) hides the button; the Addons menu opens Reliquary either way.
- **Guild chat in the window.** The Guild tab starts with your guild's chat, kept as the game showed it: each speaker's lines under their class icon, name and the time, their links opening as in chat, a day's start marked. Search it by words or name, show only today, the past week, lines that mention you, item links or achievements, and send to the guild from the box at its foot. A speaker's icon or name opens their page. After each login Reliquary reads back from the game's servers what the guild said while you were away (the newest thousand lines, as far back as the game's servers keep them), as the game's Guild & Communities window does; a line taken down there leaves Reliquary's too. Lines from the guild's Discord show under the speaker's Discord name, in the Discord channel's chat colour. It stays on your computer: it's never sent to anyone.
- **Officer chat, for officers.** A character whose rank hears officer chat gets an Officer Chat row under Guild Chat: the same view in the officer colour, read back after each login too, its box sending to officer chat. It shows to nobody else.
- **Whispers on a player's page.** Your whispers with a player, both ways, kept and searchable on their page in the window (a Whispers page once you have any; for someone who doesn't share, their page from the start), with a box to whisper them. Battle.net whispers too, under the friend's BattleTag and in the game's Battle.net whisper colour, answered by Battle.net. They stay on your computer too.
- **Every friend on the Friends tab.** Your friends list and your Battle.net friends (those online, sharing with you or with whispers), as the Guild tab lists the guild: what they share with you, else what the friends list says, and your whispers. Anyone else you've whispered is under Others you've whispered, so every conversation has a page.
- **Painted in the game's own art.** Each character's page is headed by their class's painting from the game's dressing room, with where they are, their gold and time played beside their name when they share them; each talent tree sits on the painting the game's talent window gives it, and every list shows each character's class icon.
- **No empty pages.** A tab with nothing to show for the character you're playing (Guild and Guild Bank without a guild, Public before anyone shares with everyone, Friends before you have any) is greyed and says why on hover, as the game greys a tab that doesn't apply. Each tab's title, counts and switch head its list, over a search; the Characters tab's says how many characters you have and their gold and time played between them. Every list gives each person two lines, as the friends list does: under the name, their rank and where they are or when they were last on, and under the guild's chats their last line. The guild bank has its tabs down its right side and shows each tab's latest moves under its slots, and an item's page moves its own sections beside the people around it rather than leave a column empty.
- **The game's own colours and sounds throughout**: reputation bars in each standing's colour (how far into it on hover), quests coloured by how hard they are, items in their quality's colour, and the game's sounds as you click.
- **Polish.** Chat in the game's chat font; a long name never pushes a line's time or the guild's counts out of place (the whole name shows on hover); a guild bank tab's note stays on one line, all of it on hover; Reliquary's lines under an item in the game's own tooltip colours.
- **Item pages, redrawn.** Click an item anywhere in the window and its page slides in over the tab you were on: the item and its name at the top, then cards. On the left, its own lines as its tooltip gives them, its auction price and what a vendor pays, and what it's made with, each reagent on a slot with how many you have of how many it takes (white when you have enough), and what it's used to make, each item in its quality's colour: every one a click from its own page, and Back and Escape step back through the items you opened. On the right, who in the guild can use it, then who can make it, whose recipes use it and who could learn it, each under their class icon and a click from their page, then where it comes from, each place beside the game's own map icon. Its recipes come from everyone you know (yours, your other characters' and what guildmates and friends share), not only your own notes, and an item the game hasn't fetched yet is named from the tooltip Reliquary read. Everyone an item would help is listed, however many. A recipe lists everything it is made with on hover.
- **Shift-click and Ctrl-click** on an item the game hasn't loaded yet do what you asked once it arrives: a link goes only into the chat box you had open, and only while it still is.
- **Long roleplay profiles scroll** rather than being cut short.
- The window keeps where you last dragged it, opens at the game's own panel level, and shows a fifth and sixth profession.
- **On a Mac**, an item's page names Cmd+C to copy an address.

## 1.6.0 (built 2026-11-09, released with 1.7.0)

- **Blizzard's auction prices through your guild.** When someone in your guild has Blizzard's own hourly auction prices for your realm's houses (Reliquary's dashboard reads them from Blizzard's web API once that player's auction house has been visited, and hands them to their game while it is closed), Reliquary passes them through the guild: every guildmate who uses Reliquary gets them just by being in the guild, without visiting the auction house, and whoever has them passes them on, so they keep travelling while that player is away. It works whether or not anyone shares with the guild: a guildmate who logs in is answered by whoever has newer prices, once. They name no player, so no switch holds them back; your own scans still go only when you turn on "Share prices and sources". Item pages say "Blizzard's hourly prices", and Blizzard's prices are kept thirty days at most, as Blizzard's terms ask.

## 1.5.0 (built 2026-11-09, released with 1.6.0)

- **Every recipe you know.** Open a profession's window once and Reliquary keeps every recipe that character knows: what each makes, what goes into it, and its group as the game's own window shows it. A recipe learned later, even with the window closed, joins it.
- **Your professions, shared with their recipes.** Recipes go with the Professions part of the sharing grid, to whoever you show your professions: your guild, your friends or anyone. Show only your professions and that is all that goes. In `/rq`, a guildmate's or friend's page has a Recipes page, and their profession bars open it.
- **Who can make it, who uses it.** Every item's page says who in your guild and among your friends, and which of your own characters, can make it, whose recipes use it, and for a pattern or plans who could learn it (they have the profession and the skill, and their recipes show they don't know it yet). A cooking ingredient, an enchanting material or an engineering part (as the game files it, a crafting reagent) names everyone with that profession right away, before their recipes are read.
- **A line when you loot something someone else can use.** Loot something no profession of yours uses and one line in chat says who it would help: "Faelwen can use your [Light Leather] for Leatherworking." A pattern says who can learn it. Once a day for each item; "Who can use what I loot" on the Sharing tab turns it off.
- **Plain text from other players.** Everything another player's Reliquary sends is kept as plain text: the game's codes for colours, links, textures and icons are taken out as it arrives, so nothing anyone sends can draw, link or colour anything in your window, your tooltips or your chat.
- **Lines under items on the game's tooltips**, each labelled and only when it has something to say: its auction price (Auction), who it's set aside for and who it would be an upgrade for, who in your guild, among your friends and your characters can make it (Made by), uses it in a recipe (Used by) or could learn a pattern (Can be learned by), and who else has it (Held by: your other characters, the guild bank, and guildmates who show their bags). They go through the game's own tooltip hook that every addon shares, so other addons' lines stay as they are, and only on the item tooltip and a chat link's, never the comparison tooltip beside it. "Lines on item tooltips" on the Sharing tab turns them off.

## 1.4.0 (built 2026-11-09, released with 1.5.0)

- **Every item opens its page.** Click any item in `/rq` (or on the dashboard): the game's tooltip, everyone in the guild who can equip it with the people it would help most first, where you and the guild bank have it, its auction price, where it comes from, and its Wowhead and ForeverDB addresses to copy. Ctrl-click tries it on; Shift-click links it in chat, as everywhere in the game.
- **Who can use it.** For the guild bank's items, the spares guildmates show in their bags and anything linked in guild chat, each player's own game judges for its character: whether it can use the item (the game marks what it can't in red), whether it suits its spec (Blizzard's own list of which specs an item is for, else the spec's main stat), and whether it beats what it wears in that slot. Upgrade, At level N (within five levels), Trade-off; no gain folds into one line. Guildmates who show the guild their gear share these verdicts with it; for anyone offline, your game judges from the gear they share, when Blizzard's list covers the item. A bound item never counts.
- **Handing gear out.** An officer sets a bank item aside for someone (as many as the tab holds) and can take it back; a guildmate asks for one on their own row. An ask says what the asker's own game makes of the item (Upgrade, At level N, Trade-off or No gain, never what they wear), kept current as they level or change gear, so the guild sees whether it would help them even if they don't show their gear. The whole guild sees it on the item, in the tooltip and on that guildmate's page, the moment it happens or when they next log in. It clears once the item leaves the bank, and after two weeks. Reliquary never decides who gets anything: people do.
- **A line at login**, at most one: gear set aside for you, and new upgrades for you in the guild bank (never trade-offs). It can be turned off on the Sharing tab.
- **The bank's upgrade arrow** marks what would help someone (gold when it helps you), and a **Helps someone** filter beside the search shows only those.
- **Auction prices.** When you open the auction house, Reliquary reads every listing at once with Blizzard's own full-scan call, at most once every fifteen minutes, a few hundred listings a frame, and keeps the cheapest price for one of each item and how many are listed, never who sells. With the house open, an item's page asks it about that item there and then. Prices show on game tooltips ("Auction 18s 50c each") for a week, and on item pages with whose scan it was and when; an item your newest scan didn't list says none were listed then. Every house is kept apart, on any realm, Alliance, Horde and neutral alike, and each character sees only the houses it can use: its own faction's and the neutral one on its realm.
- **Where items come from**, noted as you play: what vendors sell and for how much, what drops from which creatures and where (fishing, gathering and opened containers too), quest rewards and choices, what your recipes make and use, and which boss drops a piece of gear when the game's collection knows it.
- **Share prices and sources with my guild** (off until you turn it on): your newest scan goes to guildmates who use Reliquary, and your notes answer their questions about an item. Theirs come to you either way.
- **One Reliquary line on game tooltips**, only when it has news: the auction price, who it's set aside for, who it would be an upgrade for. It can be turned off on the Sharing tab.
- **Before Reliquary.** The first time Reliquary runs for a character, it reads once what the game still remembers from before: the Legacy challenges it completed and when, every quest it completed, the areas of each zone it uncovered on the world map, the game's lifetime statistics, lifetime PvP and titles, with its level and time played. Only challenges are dated, so nothing else is placed in time. It opens the dashboard's timeline; Legacy challenges completed from now on join the timeline where they happen.
- **Two of your characters with the same given name on one realm** keep separate records: each is kept under its full name. A record from an earlier version moves to its character's full name at its next login, with everything it held.

## 1.3.0 (built 2026-11-09, released with 1.4.0)

- **Share your character.** Each character chooses who sees it: your guild, your friends, anyone, or nobody. Everything starts off for a new character; type `/rq share` to choose, or use the Sharing tab of `/rq` or Options, AddOns, Reliquary.
- **Choose what each sees, part by part:** gear, talents, professions, character sheet, gold, where you are, time played, reputation, quest log, bags and bank, each for your guild, your friends and anyone separately. A new character shows gear, talents, professions and its character sheet, and nothing else until you say so. Name, race, class, level and guild go with any of them; photos never leave your PC. Choose on the Sharing tab of `/rq`, in Options, or with `/rq share gold public off`.
- **The window (`/rq`):** your guild with what every guildmate shares, your friends' and other players', and the guild bank as you or your guildmates last saw it.
- **Guild:** the whole roster with ranks, levels, zones, professions and public notes (never officer notes); guildmates who share show what they choose: gear and talents, and if they like where they are, gold, bags, reputation and quests.
- **Guild bank:** open it once and Reliquary reads every tab your rank may view, and guildmates who share with the guild pass on what they see. A tab goes by whisper only to guildmates whose rank may see it; only a tab open to everyone goes to the whole guild.
- **The guild master decides who sees the bank in Reliquary:** on the Guild Bank tab's Sharing panel, each tab as in game, opened wider (a rank and up, or everyone) or kept in the game only, and one switch that shares each tab's log, the gold log, the tabs' notes and what each rank may take, sent only by the ranks the guild master trusts with them (Officer and up unless chosen otherwise). Settings count only from the guild master in your own guild list, and stop counting when leadership passes.
- **Friends:** your friends list, and Battle.net friends.
- **Total RP 3:** with Total RP 3 installed, Reliquary asks it for the roleplay profile of everyone who shares with you (as Total RP 3 does when you mouse over someone, at most every fifteen minutes each, never in a fight). Each player's page in `/rq` gets a Roleplay page with their profile and a button that opens Total RP 3's own page for them; guildmates' pages show theirs too.
- **Anyone:** when you target a player who uses Reliquary and shares publicly, what they share arrives; `/rq look <name>` asks by name.
- Messages wait out fights, instances and the game's chat lockdown, and go out about one a second so they never crowd other addons. Only what changed goes again: new loot resends just your bags. Hiding a part or turning sharing off takes effect at once, even for what was already waiting to go, and tells those who had it to forget it.
- Characters recorded before this version stay public, as they were on their owner's public page.
- Each character keeps a **timeline** of where it spends its time: a stretch of 10 active minutes in one zone, or a quest handed in there, with its levels, quests and killing blows. Time marked AFK is left out, and after 30 days each zone's day folds into one line. `/rq status` counts its entries.
- `/rq` opens the window; `/rq status` prints what is recorded; `/rq help` lists the commands.
