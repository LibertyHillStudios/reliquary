Privacy

Effective November 9, 2026

Who we are

Liberty Hill Studios, LLC (“Liberty Hill Studios,” “we”) makes Reliquary, an addon for World of Warcraft, and runs its pages at reliquary.libertyhillstudio.com. This policy covers those pages. The studio’s website has its own privacy policy.

What these pages show

  • World of Warcraft characters whose player set them to share publicly in Reliquary, with only the parts the player chose to show. Gold, where a character is, time played, reputation, quests, bags and bank appear only when the player turns them on. A character set back to private leaves these pages the next time the game saves.
  • Pictures of those characters from the game.
  • Auction house prices for the items those characters show, from Blizzard Entertainment (below).

Nothing other players share in the game, and nothing of a guild’s roster or bank, appears here.

Auction prices come from Blizzard

Auction prices come from Blizzard Entertainment’s World of Warcraft API, read once an hour: the lowest price for one of each item, and how many were listed. Blizzard’s data names no sellers or buyers, and these pages hold nothing about any player’s trades. We keep the prices for thirty days at most.

In the game, Reliquary passes these prices to guildmates who use it, through the game’s own addon messages, so they have them without visiting the auction house. Nothing about any player goes with them, and the addon also keeps them thirty days at most.

The prices are provided “as is.” Blizzard makes no guarantee that the data is available or correct, and neither do we. Reliquary is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment. World of Warcraft and Blizzard Entertainment are trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.

What we keep when you visit

  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads or cookies, and we never ask for your Battle.net password or any other.
  • Our servers keep no log of who visits: no IP addresses and no pages viewed. We collect no personal information from anyone.
  • Cloudflare delivers these pages. It handles each connection, including your IP address, and counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not follow you from site to site. Cloudflare’s privacy policy covers that.
  • The page remembers, in your own browser, which character you last looked at. That never leaves your device.
  • Links to Wowhead, ForeverDB and Warcraft Wiki open their sites, under their own policies.

Your Battle.net account

Your Battle.net account and your play are covered by Blizzard’s privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and change the date above.
